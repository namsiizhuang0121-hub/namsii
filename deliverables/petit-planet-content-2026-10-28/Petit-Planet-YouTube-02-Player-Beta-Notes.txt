What Games Let You Customize Your Home or Environment? My Petit Planet Beta Notes

If you're asking, “What games let you customize your home or environment?”, Petit Planet is one I'd recommend keeping an eye on. I played the beta, and making my own little planet feel like home was one of the things I enjoyed most.

It's HoYoverse's cozy galactic life sim. You have your own small planet to build and decorate, with farming, fishing, cooking and the Starsea to explore. That combination really worked for me: a home I wanted to make my own, everyday activities I enjoyed, and somewhere beyond home to head out into.
